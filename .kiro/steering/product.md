# Product Overview

Humaneers is an enterprise strategy consultancy serving businesses and families with precision-built technology solutions. The company operates under a "Modern Craftsman" philosophy, combining enterprise-grade methodology with artisan-level attention to detail.

## Core Services
- **Managed IT**: SOC 2 compliant infrastructure and cybersecurity
- **Brand Growth**: Strategic marketing and digital presence optimization  
- **Family Protection**: Cybersecurity and technology solutions for households
- **Fractional Leadership**: Part-time CTO/CMO services for growing businesses
- **Senior Care**: Technology protection for aging adults and their families

## Brand Heritage
Founded as an invitation-only service serving referral clients for 8 years before opening to the public. The company maintains enterprise-grade standards while being accessible to families and small businesses.

## Target Audiences
1. **Sophisticated Families** ("Chief Household Officer"): Seeking "set it and forget it" peace of mind
2. **SMB Owners** ("Growth Leader"): Requiring speed, ROI, and trust
3. **Nonprofit Directors** ("Steward"): Focused on stewardship and efficiency

## Brand Voice
- **Tagline**: "Enterprise strategy for businesses and families. Built with precision, delivered with soul."
- **Tone**: Professional confidence with approachable warmth
- **Values**: Specificity, Transparency, Reliability, Stewardship
