## Executive Summary

Humaneers provides managed IT services, cybersecurity, strategic leadership, and technology consulting for businesses, households, nonprofits, and high-net-worth individuals. The company differentiates on:

- **100% US-Based Operations** — No offshore call centers
- **SOC 2 Type II Compliance** — Enterprise-grade security for all clients
- **No Vendor Lock-in** — Built on standard stacks (Microsoft, AWS)
- **Concierge Service Model** — White-glove technology management

- **Hourly Packs** — Can be used for urgent crisis response; hours never expire
