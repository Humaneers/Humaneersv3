## What Makes Humaneers Different

### The Things That Matter to Your Prospects

**100% US-Based**
No offshore call centers. Every engineer and strategist is local. When clients call, they talk to someone who knows their business by name.

**SOC 2 Compliant**
Enterprise-grade security controls applied to every client, big or small. We apply the same zero-trust architecture to a solo founder's laptop as we do to a fifty-person corporate network.

**No Vendor Lock-in**
We build on standard stacks (Microsoft, AWS) so clients own their infrastructure. We sell solutions, not licenses. If open-source software is better for a client's budget than a proprietary subscription, that's what we recommend. No preferred partners. No kickbacks.

**Radical Transparency**
Clients own their data. They own their code. They own their strategy. We document everything so they never feel tied to our services by obscurity.

**Aligned Incentives**
Most MSPs operate on a broken model: they profit when you have problems. We operate on a subscription model—we only profit when systems are running perfectly.
